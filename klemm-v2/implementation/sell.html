The Show-Ready Sale

Six steps. A clear plan at every stage.

  1. 01

    The walk-through

    Jack walks your house and gives you two honest numbers: what it's worth as-is, and what it's worth show-ready. Then he helps you decide which repairs and upgrades make sense for your goals. No pressure toward either path.

  2. 02

    He runs the work

    Say yes, and Jack gathers the bids and puts his own contractors, cleaners, and stagers to work. He schedules them and supervises them — you don't have to be in town, or in California.

  3. 03

    Staged like a model home

    Buyers pay for what they can picture themselves living in. Your house gets the model-home treatment: staged rooms, professional photography, virtual tours, and a listing built to stop the scroll.

  4. 04

    Priced from real data

    Jack has helped local buyers and sellers since 1988. He prices from what your street actually sells for — not a ZIP-code average — to draw strong offers in the first week, when buyer attention is highest.

  5. 05

    Marketing and showings

    Your listing goes out with professional photos across Zillow and the MLS, in front of the buyer agents Jack has worked with for decades. He handles every showing and every call. Sellers routinely never attend a single one.

  6. 06

    Escrow to sold

    Jack negotiates the offers, manages inspections and paperwork, and walks the sale to closing. From the first bid to the final signature, his is the only phone number you need.
