Tracy’s broker since 1988

The same streets.
The same person to call.

Jack Klemm started selling homes in Tracy in 1988, and he never left. He’s watched this market through the booms and the slowdowns, helping people buy and sell on the streets he knows.

Klemm Real Estate is his own independent brokerage. When you call, you get Jack — the same person who walks your house, prices it, lines up the contractors and stagers, and negotiates your contract.

For sellers, that starts with a walk-through and an honest look at what the house is worth as-is or show-ready. Jack helps decide which improvements make sense, gathers the bids and oversees the work. He stays involved through the inspections, paperwork and final signature.

Today he works across Tracy, Mountain House, Manteca, Lathrop and River Islands. He still writes his own newsletter, sharing a local view of what’s selling.
