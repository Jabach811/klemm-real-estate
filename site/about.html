Tracy's broker since 1988

Thirty-eight years on the same streets.

Jack Klemm started selling homes in Tracy in 1988, and he never left. Nearly four thousand closings later, he's watched this market through every boom and every slowdown — and he can tell you what your street sold for in any of those years, usually from memory.

Klemm Real Estate is his own independent brokerage: no franchise playbook, no team with his name on the door. When you call, you get Jack — the same person who walks your house, prices it, lines up the contractors and stagers, and negotiates your contract. Nothing gets handed off, because there's no one to hand it to.

His clients notice. Across 123 Zillow reviews he holds a perfect 5.0 — and the thing they mention most isn't the sold price. It's that he answered the phone.

Today Jack works five communities — Tracy, Mountain House, Manteca, Lathrop, and River Islands — and still writes his own monthly newsletter on what's selling and for how much.
