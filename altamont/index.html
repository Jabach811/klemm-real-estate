    The Altamont Advantage · 03

    A listing launch is not a listing upload.

    The difference is not a trick. It is a sequence of good decisions, made early enough to matter—and one person responsible for every handoff.

    1. 01

      Make the plan

      Walk the home, identify what earns its keep, and put the rest on the “do not bother” list.

    2. 02

      Make it show-ready

      Coordinate the repairs, cleaning, staging, photography, and tour so sellers do not have to run a small production company.

    3. 03

      Make people stop

      Launch with a clear point of view: the home, the neighborhood, and the life a buyer can picture in both.

    4. 04

      Make the offer count

      Use local judgment to weigh the whole offer—not merely the loudest number on the page.
