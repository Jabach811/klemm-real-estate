# City workspace

Each city is self-contained:

- `index.html` — the city landing page, when one has been built.
- `assets/` — every image, video, local note, and neighborhood-specific item for that city.

Current city pages: Tracy, Manteca, and Mountain House. Lathrop and River Islands currently contain their available city assets; add their future page as `index.html` in the matching folder.
