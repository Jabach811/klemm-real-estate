# Channel audit (September 2, 2026)

## The one-line version
Jack has been consistent for five years and has nothing set up around the videos. The videos are fine as proof of work. The channel around them is empty, so YouTube doesn't know who to show them to, and viewers who land on one have nowhere to go next.

## The numbers (from data/summary.json)
- 838 videos. 786 of them are titled with just the address. 49 newer ones use MLS-style remarks plus the address.
- Where they are: Tracy 496, Manteca 206, Mountain House 53, Lathrop 48 (plus 3 tagged River Islands), everything else under 10 each.
- Views: about 29,000 total across all 838. The median video has 17 views. 268 videos have fewer than 10. Only 46 have 100 or more.
- 176 videos posted in the last 12 months. Median views for those: 21. So the recent pace is high and the reach per video is low.
- 74 subscribers. That's normal for a listing-only channel. Nobody subscribes to tours of houses they aren't buying.

## What's missing on the channel itself
- No banner image. The channel header is blank.
- Default handle (@jackklemm3607). Not a name anyone would type.
- One link (klemmre.com). None of the city sites, no phone link.
- No channel keywords (the hidden words YouTube uses to figure out what a channel is about).
- No channel trailer or featured video.
- Zero playlists. 838 videos and no way to browse by city or by sold vs. for sale.
- No Shorts, no community posts. Fine for now, just noting it.

## What's missing on every video (checked several, old and new)
- No tags.
- No end screen (the clickable panel in the last 20 seconds that says "watch this next").
- No cards, no chapters.
- Comments turned off.
- Description has phone, email and DRE number, but no clickable link to the listing or the website. The hashtags are separated by commas, which is harmless but sloppy.
- Category is "People & Blogs."
- Titles: old ones are bare addresses. New ones are MLS remarks with typos ("freshly paint," "Beautifullu"). Neither says "home for sale" or leads with the city, which is what people actually search.
- Thumbnails are auto-picked exterior frames. Not bad, just inconsistent.

## What the videos are
- Recent format: 1 minute 39 seconds, a photo slideshow with slow zooms and captions, opening on a "Klemm Real Estate Presents" card.
- Older format (2021): 4 to 5 minutes, more photos, sometimes a real walkthrough.

## What actually got views
The top of the list is all older, longer videos:
- 2700 Annette Ct, Tracy: 2,000 views (4:44, 2021)
- 1295 Como Dr, Manteca: 1,200 views (4:14)
- Live at Ellis, Tracy: 772 views (1:10). This is a neighborhood video, not a listing.
- 3996 Castellina Way, Manteca: 737 views (4:10)
- 2834 Redbridge Rd, Tracy: 458 views (4:20)

Two lessons. Longer walkthroughs get 20 to 100 times the views of the 1:39 slideshows. And the only non-listing video he ever made is his third most-watched.

## What YouTube thinks the audience is
On his video page, YouTube recommended wallpaper and screensaver videos next to his tour. It has no idea who to show his content to. On his most popular video, it recommended "Why Tracy CA is INCREDIBLE," "Tracy Hills FULL TOUR," and a broker tour video. That's the neighborhood: area content from other agents. Nobody local is doing it well yet.

## Why this matters for the business
The channel has two jobs, and neither is "get subscribers."
1. Each listing video should be findable by the people already looking at that home (buyers searching the address, the seller showing friends), and it should send them to the listing page on klemmre.com.
2. A small set of area videos (city tours, monthly market updates) should catch people searching "moving to Tracy" or "Mountain House homes." Those people become buyers and sellers.
Also: "every listing gets a video" is a seller-facing selling point. The site should say so and show it.
